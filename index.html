Source: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, Hazard Event Location (HazEL); data read live from public ArcGIS Online Feature Services (earthquakes, tsunamis, eruptions).

Limitations: These datasets are not a complete list of hazard events worldwide. Historical records vary in completeness and reliability across time and place, and older or remote events are more likely to be missing or imprecise. “Not reported” means the value is absent from the record, not zero.
